## Full Summary

Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, bare coding prompts (T1 path search, T2 sorting, T3 weighted shortest path, new T4 k-th smallest). Tests whether a ban's prompt-suffix displacement Delta_h = h(ban)-h(placebo) (5 chat-template suffix tokens, layers 32-34 chosen by probe) predicts which ideas gain share. Seven one-scalar predictors (A IIA, B Delta_h displacement, C nested geometry, D/D2 bge embeddings, D3 Qwen name lexical, E co-mention) share one floor+compliance model, are fit by LOBO on 27 screen bans, FROZEN by sha256+git commit, then scored on 5,930 never-generated vLLM confirmation generations (n=100/cell). RESULTS (re-derived independently in verify_headline.py): C2a PASS - regularity violations in 6 bans (T1 Bellman-Ford, Greedy best-first, IDDFS; T2 Counting, Radix, Shell; 8/46 Holm cells), 0/45 on placebo-vs-placebo and 0 in 20 shuffled reps; e.g. banning near-zero-rate greedy best-first moves A* 3%->58%, BFS 63%->23%. Sign accuracy B 0.83 (balanced 0.79) vs IIA 0.65 (0.50). C2b FAIL - B vs IIA mean JSD on T1-T3 bans 0.188 vs 0.207 (9.2% reduction, CI [-0.6%,19.6%], target 20%), top-hit 0.20; B beats external bge embeddings D/D2 (CIs exclude 0); T4 pure transfer B worse than IIA (-25%). C2c FAIL - adding Delta_h at the suffix (prefill, layer 32), restore, 35-layer suffix ceiling and Stolfo all-position add all give recovery ~0 (mean R -0.001; random 5-draw -0.006). Exploratory decode-time attention knockout of the ban sentence restores placebo behaviour (mean restoration 0.72): the ban is read from the sentence tokens during generation, not from the prompt-final state. Logit lens of Delta_h: top tokens 'while/although/another/new', banned name median rank ~17.6k (no priming).
